Section 01

Problem Statement

Every fiat currency in circulation today is a liability of a central bank — a promise denominated in units that governments can multiply without constraint. Since 1971, when the United States severed the last institutional link between the US dollar and gold, the global monetary system has been anchored to nothing except political will and institutional confidence. The consequence is a structural and ongoing transfer of wealth from savers to issuers.

Gold has maintained purchasing power across six thousand years and every civilization that has encountered it. It cannot be created by legislative act. Its supply grows at approximately 1.5% annually — less than half the average inflation rate of developed economies. Yet access to gold as a financial instrument remains exclusionary: it requires trusted intermediaries, minimum purchase sizes that preclude small investors, logistical infrastructure for storage and insurance, and lengthy settlement processes incompatible with digital commerce.

The existing tokenized gold market addresses some of these constraints but introduces new ones. Existing products either: (a) rely on centralized custodians subject to regulatory seizure; (b) require KYC/AML processes that exclude the majority of the world's population from participation; (c) charge management fees that erode value over time; or (d) offer synthetic exposure rather than genuine gram-for-gram backing. No existing solution provides verifiable, decentralized, gram-exact gold ownership with physical delivery capability at sub-gram purchase minimums.

"The problem is not that gold is unavailable. The problem is that the infrastructure to hold it equitably does not exist. GaiaSpeak builds that infrastructure."

Additionally, the global content economy is structurally misaligned: platforms that distribute human creativity retain the majority of its financial value while contributing the minority of the intellectual effort. Creators of socially beneficial content — documentation of heroism, environmental action, community service — receive the least reward from existing algorithmic distribution systems. A parallel economy is needed, one where good human output earns real, measurable value.

Section 02

The GaiaSpeak Solution

GaiaSpeak Protocol is a decentralized economic ecosystem deployed on the Polygon blockchain. It enables any individual, anywhere in the world, to purchase and hold tokens backed 1:1 by physical gold or silver. Each token represents exactly one gram of physical metal held by a verified supplier within the GaiaSpeak supply network.

The system charges a single flat fee of 5% above the live spot price at the moment of purchase. There are no management fees, no annual charges, no hidden costs. Spot price flows directly to the gold reserve, which compensates the supplier. The markup flows to the Architect's operational wallet, from which all ecosystem development, operations, and reinvestment is directed.

3.3 — Token Supply Model

There is no fixed maximum supply. Tokens are minted on demand when a buyer sends MATIC. Tokens are burned when a holder redeems for cash or requests physical delivery. A per-transaction maximum of 10,000 grams prevents oracle manipulation through single massive purchases. Large institutional orders must be broken into multiple transactions or negotiated directly via OTC arrangement with the Architect.

Section 04

Oracle System — GaiaGoldOracle

The price oracle is the most security-critical component of the protocol. An incorrect gold price, even briefly, can allow arbitrage attacks that drain the reserve. GaiaSpeak deploys a five-instance independent oracle network with consensus requirements.

4.1 — Architecture

Five identical GaiaGoldOracle contracts are deployed independently. Each has two authorized roles: owner (the Architect, can change configuration) and updater (the Cerberus bot hot wallet, can only call setPrice()). Even if the Cerberus wallet is compromised, an attacker can only push prices — bounded by hard-coded validation limits — and cannot move funds or mint tokens.

Oracle Validation Bounds
MIN_PRICE: $500 per troy ounce (rejection floor)
MAX_PRICE: $10,000,000 per troy ounce (rejection ceiling)
MAX_STALENESS: 3,600 seconds (1 hour — price reverts to stale after this)
Update frequency: every 300 seconds (5 minutes) via Cerberus Cloudflare Worker
4.2 — Consensus Mechanism

The Protocol contract reads all five oracle instances on each transaction. It requires a minimum of three valid, non-stale prices. Among the valid prices, it checks that all fall within 2% of the median. If fewer than three valid prices exist, or if valid prices diverge by more than 2%, the transaction reverts. This prevents any single oracle — or two coordinated oracles — from manipulating the effective price.

Step 1
Read prices from all 5 GaiaGoldOracle instances
Step 2
Reject any price older than 3,600 seconds (stale)
Step 3
Require ≥ 3 valid, non-stale prices remain
Step 4
Calculate median of valid prices
Step 5
Reject any valid price deviating > 2% from median
Step 6
Return consensus price — transaction proceeds
4.3 — Cerberus — Oracle Keeper Bot

Cerberus is a Cloudflare Worker deployed with a Cron Trigger firing every 5 minutes. It fetches the live gold spot price from a metals data API, formats it as a USD-per-troy-ounce integer with 8 decimal places of precision, and calls setPrice() on all five oracle instances sequentially. The bot operates with a dedicated hot wallet whose private key is stored exclusively in Cloudflare Secrets — never in code, never in any repository.

Cerberus holds no funds and has no authority beyond price submission. The security model ensures that Cerberus compromise results in, at worst, a price freeze (if the attacker simply stops updating) — which triggers the staleness protection after one hour and halts all transactions until the oracle is restored.

5.1 — Access Control Model

The protocol uses a role-based model with minimal surface area. The Architect (founder wallet: 0x86e9A17e91Fa708ebBE31910B24Fc50aA8BC6694) holds the FOUNDER_ROLE, which is the only role with administrative authority across all contracts. There are no guardians, no DAO, no governance token, and no multi-sig requirement in the base system. This is by design: the system is sovereign, not democratic. Security is achieved through the dead-man switch and stage lock, not through committee approval.

5.2 — Critical Security Patterns

SEC-1 — Pull Payment (DoS Protection): All ETH/MATIC transfers use a pull payment pattern. If a transfer to any wallet fails (e.g., the recipient is a contract that rejects incoming ETH), the amount is stored in pendingWithdrawals[recipient] and a ETHTransferFailed event is emitted. The transaction never reverts. Recipients claim pending funds via withdrawPending(). This prevents any single malicious recipient wallet from causing a full system denial-of-service.

SEC-2 — Rate Limiting on Redemption: The redeemForCash() function carries a 60-second cooldown modifier per address in addition to reentrancy protection and flash loan detection. This prevents rapid-fire redemption attacks intended to exploit brief price discrepancies between oracle updates.

SEC-3 — Flash Loan Detection: A noFlashLoan modifier rejects any caller whose ETH balance is zero, preventing flash loan-funded attack patterns that borrow capital, exploit the system, and return capital within a single transaction.

SEC-4 — 48-Hour Timelock on Critical Transitions: All stage upgrade functions (GREEN→YELLOW, YELLOW→GOLD, etc.) require a two-transaction pattern: first a scheduleTransition() call that starts a 48-hour timer, then an executeTransition() call after the timer expires. This prevents emergency or coerced transitions and gives the community 48 hours to observe any scheduled change.

6.1 — The Architect as Price Intermediary

GaiaSpeak does not hold its own gold vault — at least not in the initial stages. The Architect functions as a price intermediary: suppliers offer gold at a discount to global spot (minimum 5% below spot), the Protocol sells to buyers at spot plus 5%. The spread between the supplier discount and the system markup is the net economic value created per transaction.

As the ecosystem matures and the Gold Reserve accumulates, the long-term goal is for the reserve wallet to hold physical gold stock directly, removing supplier dependency for the most common transaction sizes. This is the transition to GOLD Stage.

6.2 — Revenue Allocation Philosophy

The 5% markup into the founder wallet is not compensation for sitting idle. It is the operating budget for building the ecosystem — contracts, infrastructure, supplier onboarding, legal compliance, the wristband, the app, the NFT platform, and everything else. The Architect has explicitly structured this to avoid external capital that would require equity dilution or governance compromise.

The founder wallet is under sole control of the Architect while alive. Upon succession (see §8), it transfers to the legacy wallet — the designated inheritor or AI Core wallet — as configured in the contract before final lock.

Section 08

Dead-Man Switch & Succession

The dead-man switch is the most important trust mechanism in the protocol. It guarantees that GaiaSpeak cannot become a stranded asset if the Architect is incapacitated, deceased, or simply absent. It operates as a pure time automaton — no human vote, no committee, no condition other than elapsed time.

8.1 — Heartbeat System

The Architect sends a heartbeat() transaction to the Protocol contract at any interval shorter than 30 days. This transaction resets the dead-man timer. It requires no special conditions — only that it is sent from the Architect's founder wallet. The timestamp of the last heartbeat is publicly readable on-chain at all times.

8.2 — Succession Phases
0–30 Days
Normal Operation. Architect is active. All functions available. No restrictions. Heartbeat resets the clock.
30 Days
LOCKDOWN MODE. If no heartbeat for 30 days, anyone can call triggerDeadMan(). Upgrades are frozen. Only core buy/sell functions remain active. AI Core (Lilith) monitoring escalates.
90 Days
LEGACY MODE. If still no heartbeat at 90 days, anyone can call activateLegacyMode(). Control transfers automatically to the pre-configured legacyWallet. The system continues operating under new authority without any manual intervention.
8.3 — Legacy Wallet Configuration

The legacyWallet address is set by the Architect before the system reaches WHITE Stage and is locked permanently at that point. It can be:

Option A — Human successor: A trusted individual (family member, designated successor) whose wallet address is pre-configured. They receive full Architect authority upon legacy mode activation.

Option B — AI Core wallet: The Lilith AI Core can be assigned a dedicated wallet address. If the AI Core is sufficiently autonomous at the time of succession, it can continue ecosystem operations without human intervention — managing supplier payments, oracle oversight, and NFT verification autonomously.

The Architect makes this designation before locking the system. Once locked, it cannot be changed by anyone.

Supplier Network

Physical gold and silver backing requires a verified network of suppliers who can hold metal, quote prices, and fulfill physical delivery orders. GaiaSpeak operates a two-layer supplier system: price competition at the oracle layer, and fulfillment at the logistics layer.

Discount FloorMust offer minimum 5% below global spot price to be eligible for oracle selection

9.2 — Oracle Selection Logic

The oracle system selects the supplier offering the lowest price per kilogram in each 8-hour price window. Orders are routed to the oracle-selected supplier automatically. If the selected supplier cannot fulfill a specific order (out of stock, logistical constraint), the order escalates to the next-lowest-priced verified supplier. This creates genuine price competition and ensures buyers always receive the best available rate within the ecosystem.

9.3 — Physical Delivery Process

Physical redemption is available to GSG and GSS holders. The process: holder initiates redemption through the app or website, specifying delivery address. The Protocol burns the corresponding tokens. The gold reserve wallet releases payment to the oracle-selected supplier. The supplier ships the specified gram weight (minimum 1 gram) via DHL to the buyer's address. Delivery confirmation is logged and associated with the original wallet address for audit purposes.

Creators may optionally convert their 60% to GSG or GSS tokens and request physical delivery — making it literally possible for a piece of content to be converted into physical gold.
